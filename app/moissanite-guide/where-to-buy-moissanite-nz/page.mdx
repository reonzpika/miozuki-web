## How to judge any moissanite retailer: questions to ask yourself

When you're looking at any retailer, ask these questions. They work regardless of which store you're considering.

**Do they clearly explain what moissanite is?** Moissanite is a gemstone in its own right. Jewellery-grade moissanite is lab-grown. Look for clear stone and setting details rather than relying on a diamond comparison alone. [Learn more about moissanite itself](/moissanite-guide).

**Can you see the actual stones or rings?** Some retailers let you try before you buy (physical visits, try-at-home programs, video consultations). Others are online-only. Neither is wrong, but you need to know which one you're getting. If you want to see fire and glow in daylight, or try a ring's weight on your hand, physical access matters.

**What are the shipping and return terms?** Check production time separately from dispatch and delivery. Ask whether the selected size, engraving or custom work affects return or resizing eligibility.

**Do they offer custom design?** If you have a specific vision, can they build it? Most retailers offer some level of customisation. How long does it take? Two weeks or ten?

**Are gold options clear and live-priced?** If you are looking for a gold moissanite ring, check whether gold is actually selectable on the product page or whether it needs a custom quote. Some jewellers show gold as standard. Others treat gold as a custom upgrade, which can make the final price less clear until you ask. A good buying experience should make the metal choice easy to understand before checkout: sterling silver, 9k gold or 9ct gold, white gold, yellow gold, rose gold, and any extra lead time attached to that choice.

**What documentation comes with the stone?** Ask who issued it and which piece it covers. Supplier specifications, brand warranties and independent laboratory reports are different. [Read more on moissanite grades](/moissanite-guide/moissanite-grades-and-brands-nz).

**Do they explain everyday care?** Ask how to clean the stone and care for the particular metal and finish. A ring worn every day needs care even when its gemstone is durable.

**What supports their environmental claims?** Lab-grown origin alone does not establish an environmental or labour guarantee for the finished piece. Look for evidence that explains the stone, metal and manufacturing claims separately.

**Do they have a voice?** You're choosing not just a ring, but a relationship. Do they feel like people you want to work with? Some retailers are warm and personal. Others are transactional. Both can deliver great rings. Which one do you want to buy from?
