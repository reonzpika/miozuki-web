## How to judge any moissanite retailer: questions to ask yourself

When you're looking at any retailer, ask these questions. They work regardless of which store you're considering.

**Do they clearly explain what moissanite is?** A good retailer tells you it's lab-grown, durable (Mohs 9.25), and ethical, and does NOT call it a "fake diamond" or "diamond substitute". If their copy treats moissanite as "diamond for less", move on. Moissanite stands on its own. [Learn more about moissanite itself](/moissanite-guide).

**Can you see the actual stones or rings?** Some retailers let you try before you buy (physical visits, try-at-home programs, video consultations). Others are online-only. Neither is wrong, but you need to know which one you're getting. If you want to see fire and glow in daylight, or try a ring's weight on your hand, physical access matters.

**What's their shipping and returns policy?** Free shipping is standard now. But look at returns: do they offer 30, 60, or 100 days? Do they refund, exchange, or store credit only? A generous return window reflects confidence in both product and fit.

**Do they offer custom design?** If you have a specific vision, can they build it? Most retailers offer some level of customisation. How long does it take? Two weeks or ten?

**What's their stone guarantee?** Do they certify moissanite grades (Forever One, Harro, NEO)? Can you see a cert? If not, ask why. [Read more on moissanite grades](/moissanite-guide/moissanite-grades-and-brands-nz).

**Do they talk about durability honestly?** The best retailers address the clouding myth directly: moissanite does not cloud with daily wear. If they gloss over this or leave it vague, that's a red flag.

**How do they position ethics?** Do they talk about lab-grown, conflict-free, sustainable metals? Or just "like a diamond but cheaper"? The first suggests they've thought through why moissanite matters. The second suggests they see it as a downmarket product.

**Do they have a voice?** You're choosing not just a ring, but a relationship. Do they feel like people you want to work with? Some retailers are warm and personal. Others are transactional. Both can deliver great rings. Which one do you want to buy from?
