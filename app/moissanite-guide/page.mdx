Moissanite is a lab-grown gemstone known for its brilliance and durability. It
was first discovered in a meteorite crater, and today it is made in a lab, so
every stone is created without mining. For a lot of New Zealand buyers it is the
considered alternative to a mined diamond: bright, hard-wearing, and far better
value for the size.

This guide walks through what moissanite is, how it compares to the
alternatives, and how to choose one with confidence.
