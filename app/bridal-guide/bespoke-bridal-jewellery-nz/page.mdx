# Bespoke bridal jewellery in New Zealand: the Miozuki guide

<GuideSchema title="Bespoke Bridal Jewellery NZ: Design Process" path="/bridal-guide/bespoke-bridal-jewellery-nz" updated="2026-10-16" />

If you have an idea for bridal jewellery that you cannot find in the current collection, start with an enquiry. Tell Miozuki what you have in mind and ask which custom options are available before making plans around a particular design or wedding date.

This guide helps you prepare that conversation. The available options, process, price, payment terms, timing and any aftercare should be confirmed for your particular enquiry before you order.

## Start with fit and how the piece will look when worn

Ring fit and how large a stone looks on the hand are two questions Miozuki customers often ask. Both matter when preparing a custom enquiry: a carat label alone does not show how a ring will sit or feel on your hand.

Include your ring size if you know it, the stone shape you prefer, and any dimensions that matter to you. If you are unsure about sizing, use the [size guide](/pages/size-guide) before deciding. For help comparing shapes and proportions, see [how to choose a moissanite ring](/moissanite-guide/how-to-choose-a-moissanite-ring).

## What to include in your enquiry

| Detail | What to share or ask |
|---|---|
| The piece | Ring, earrings, necklace, or another idea. Ask whether it is available as a custom option. |
| Appearance | Preferred stone shape, approximate dimensions, metal colour and setting style. Inspiration images can help explain what you mean. |
| Fit | Known ring size or preferred necklace length, plus any comfort concerns. |
| Everyday wear | How you expect to wear the piece and any activities that affect your choice of materials. |
| Budget | The amount you are comfortable spending, so available options can be discussed. |
| Wedding date | When you need the piece, allowing time to confirm production and delivery separately. |

## Confirm the process before ordering

Ask what information can be provided before you decide, how approval works, and which details will be confirmed in writing.

Before placing an order, confirm:

- Which requested features are possible and the final product specification.
- The quote, what it includes, and the payment terms.
- The expected production, dispatch and delivery timing for your address.
- Whether changes are possible after approval and which order terms apply.
- What aftercare is available and whether adjustments or other services carry a charge.

If you want to use an existing stone or adapt jewellery you already own, ask whether that work can be accepted. Availability should be confirmed before you rely on it.

## Moissanite and pearl: consider the whole piece

**Moissanite** is a lab-grown gemstone. GIA describes it as Mohs 9.25, less hard than diamond. Scratch resistance does not mean it cannot be damaged, and the metal, finish and setting need care too. Read [what moissanite is](/moissanite-guide) and the [durability guide](/moissanite-guide/moissanite-durability-nz) when considering daily wear.

**Pearls** need gentler handling. Consider how often the piece will encounter water, cosmetics, friction or impact, and ask which materials and settings suit your intended use. Read the [pearl guide](/pearl-guide) and [pearl care advice](/pearl-guide/pearl-care).

A custom order does not by itself establish environmental or labour standards. Ask what information is available for the particular gemstone, metal and finished piece.

## Compare custom options with the current collection

A piece from the current collection may already suit your preferred style. Compare its listed dimensions, metal options, availability and timing with any custom quote before deciding.

Miozuki does not currently sell premade bridal sets. Choose individual pieces to build your own combination. The [bridal jewellery sets guide](/bridal-guide/bridal-jewellery-sets-nz) explains how to coordinate them, and the [bridal earrings guide](/bridal-guide/bridal-earrings-nz) covers scale, neckline and comfort.
